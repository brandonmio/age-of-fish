Three changes that mattered

Jaws appear

Fish with jaws show up in the later Silurian. Jaws let animals bite, grip, and crush, which opened up whole new ways of feeding.

Bone and armor

Plates of bone covered the heads and bodies of many Devonian fish, from small bottom dwellers to predators like Dunkleosteus.

Fins become limbs

Lobe-finned fish such as Eusthenopteron and Tiktaalik had fins with bones inside, a step toward the legs of land animals.
